01 / RESEARCH PROBLEM

Understanding student dropout risk.

Student dropout is an important educational challenge. This research investigates whether machine-learning techniques can identify useful patterns associated with students who leave their studies.

WHY IT MATTERS

Early identification can support better decisions.

When students leave education before completing their studies, the effects can extend beyond the individual student to educational institutions and wider society.

Data mining provides an opportunity to investigate historical student information and determine whether meaningful patterns can help distinguish students with different academic outcomes.
